Welcome to OmniJump

Give any page a short keyword, then type that keyword in your address bar to jump straight to it. Here's the whole trick:

gh
→ jumps to github.com
1

Save a keyword

Click the OmniJump icon in your toolbar — the current page and a suggested keyword are pre-filled. You can also right-click any page or link and choose Save Page as Shortcut.

2

Type it in the address bar

Type the keyword — like gh — where you'd normally search, and press Enter. That's it. You jump.

3

Go further

Search shortcuts take an argument: save yt with a %s template and yt lofi beats searches YouTube directly. Bundles open several tabs at once — perfect for a morning routine.

Good to know

  • Can't remember a keyword? Type oj then a space in the address bar to browse and autocomplete your shortcuts.
  • Keyword also a real search? Add any second word (except on search shortcuts) and OmniJump stays out of the way.
  • Manage everything in the side panel — press Ctrl+Shift+S or click "Open full panel" in the popup.

Jumping works when your browser's default search engine is Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Brave, Kagi, Ecosia, or Startpage. The oj keyword works with any search engine.

You can reopen the panel any time from the toolbar icon.
